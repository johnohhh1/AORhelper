## The ChiliHead Commitment Framework

### 1. Sense of Belonging
**A genuine connection with your TMs**
- How are you making a genuine connection with your TMs?

### 2. Clear Direction
**Set expectations with the whys**
- In what ways are you coaching within your AO to impact the 4 Key Results?

### 3. Preparation & Support
**Teach and Coach**
- How are you providing Preparation & Support to the TMs within your AO?

### 4. Accountability
**Own what you can control**
- In what ways do you show ownership & accountability within your AO?
